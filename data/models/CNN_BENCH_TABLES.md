# CNN-bench tables

Generated by `python_scripts/cnn_bench/report.py` from the per-dataset `manifest.csv` / `validation.csv`. Accuracies are top-1 of the exported ONNX model on the full test split (onnxruntime); *CSV acc* is on the 100 held-out rows of `<dataset>_test100.csv`; *N2 check* is Network2 (spexplain's `onnx-eval`) vs onnxruntime on those rows.

## Architectures (shared by all datasets)

Split by channel width, then by pooling kind; within each group the models are ordered smallest first (ReLU count ascending). `AB<d>` / `NAB<d>` are trained on mnist/gtsrb/cifar only, so they are absent from the Imagenette-64 tables and show `--` in the cross-dataset summary.

`S<d>` / `AB<d>` -- the original family, 8/16/32 channels.

*No pooling.* `S<d>` / `NS<d>` -- downsampling is done by stride-2 convolutions, so there is no pooling layer at all and nothing beyond the ReLUs to encode.
